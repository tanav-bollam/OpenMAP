## H2. Label ambiguity: errors often name another genre the track is also tagged with

**Verdict: not testable on this dataset.** Only 0.0% of the test tracks carry more than one top-level genre tag: FMA's `fma_medium` subset contains only tracks with a single top-level genre (in the full FMA metadata, about half of all tracks have several). So no misclassification can name 'another tag of the same track', and this check cannot support or refute label ambiguity. A side effect worth knowing: because of this selection, genre labels in FMA-medium are cleaner than in the full collection, so real-world ambiguity is probably underestimated. H4 and H5 below are the available evidence about overlap.

- Among tracks both get wrong, they predict the SAME wrong genre 70.0% of the time (expected if independent given their prediction frequencies: 13.1%).

**Verdict: supported** (criterion: shared-wrong-label rate above 1.5x the independence expectation). Shared mistakes of two very different models point to ambiguous tracks or labels rather than model-specific weaknesses.
