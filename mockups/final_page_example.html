What we learned

It works
  • Pretraining pays off: AST > MERT > CNN, consistently across seeds.
  • Large, distinctive genres (Rock, Electronic, Hip-Hop, Classical) are recognised well by every model.
Limits
  • Blues, Easy Listening and Soul-RnB have too few training tracks (13–94) and stay near zero.
  • Training AST for 10 epochs overfit and scored lower than 3 epochs.
Data: FMA-medium, official artist-separated split. Test set read once per run; model selection on validation only. Full details in reports/results.md.
